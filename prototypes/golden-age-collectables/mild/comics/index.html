New Comics Every Wednesday

Wednesday is new comic book day, and we’ve celebrated it for decades. Every week we stock the latest issues from Marvel, DC, Image, and more — plus trade paperbacks and new graphic novels.

Don’t see your title on the rack? Call us to reserve a copy and we’ll set one aside: 206-622-9799.

Vintage & Rare Finds

As the world’s oldest comic shop, we know vintage. Browse graded comics, vintage Star Wars toys, Japanese and retro toys, movie scripts, autographs, posters, and collectibles you won’t find anywhere else.

Our rarest pieces never make it online — you have to see them in person. Ask at the counter and we’ll show you what’s new in the cases.

Manga & Graphic Novels

From shonen classics to the newest releases, our manga section keeps growing. We also carry a deep selection of graphic novels and trade paperbacks — superhero epics, indie favorites, and everything in between.

Beyond the page: Funko Pop figures, figurines, board games, novelties, and costumes round out the full pop culture emporium.
