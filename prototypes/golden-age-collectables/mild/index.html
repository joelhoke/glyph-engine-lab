Est. 1961

Our Story

Founded in 1961 and tucked into the lower levels of Pike Place Market, Golden Age Collectables is thought to be the oldest comic book shop in the world. Owner Rod Dyke bought the shop in 1971 as a 19-year-old comics fan — and he still drops by every Wednesday to chat with customers he’s known for three generations.
