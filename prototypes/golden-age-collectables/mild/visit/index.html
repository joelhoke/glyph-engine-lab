How to Find Us

We’re in the DownUnder — the lower levels of Pike Place Market at 1501 Pike Place. Look for the Main Arcade entrance under the iconic Public Market Center sign and head downstairs.

The selfie-ready Batman statue outside marks the spot.

Parking & Transit

Parking: The Pike Place Market parking garage at 1531 Western Ave offers skybridge access directly into the Market.

Transit: Multiple bus routes serve the Market, and the Link light rail stops at Westlake Center — a short walk away.

Accessibility: The Market has elevators serving the DownUnder levels.

Every 4th Friday

Events: Night Shift at the Market

Come join us for Pike Place Market’s Night Shift — the after-hours art & music series, every 4th Friday, July through October. The shop stays open until 9pm.

It’s the Market like you’ve never seen it: art, music, and late-night browsing in the DownUnder.
